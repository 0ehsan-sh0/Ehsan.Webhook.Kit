Task 08 — Claim storage, retry policy, and retention

Complete

Evidence

  • ADR 0008 records three total attempts, two-second initial delay, multiplier two, jitter, lease claims, and Redis retention defaults.
  • In-memory, Redis, EF Core, worker, and retry suites cover owner protection, recovery, terminal transitions, and bounded retries.
  • Redis options and EF schema mappings are present with the documented seven-day and thirty-day defaults.

Boundary

Unknown exceptions are terminal. Only explicit retryable exceptions receive another attempt, and queue admission failure does not delete a persisted record.
